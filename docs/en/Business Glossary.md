# Business Glossary - Decentralized Digital Library Project

## Project Description
The project consists of developing a decentralized digital library of digitized works. Each library user can propose digital files and request their sharing. The system manages intellectual property through moderation by librarians.

---

## Business Glossary

### A
- **AUP (Agile Unified Process)**: Development method combining agile methods and the Y-cycle, proposed by Scott Ambler.

### B
- **Bibliothécaires (Librarians)**: People dedicated to the administration and maintenance of a library, responsible for work moderation.
- **Digital Library of Works**: A library usable through a digital device allowing access to works in electronic format.
- **Heterogeneous Pairs**: Association of students with different skills and backgrounds for collaborative work.

### C
- **Work Classification**: Organization of content into categories (Books, Music, Videos, Articles) with possibility of multiple membership.
- **CultureDiffusion**: Association wishing to create the decentralized digital library.

### D
- **Request**: The action of making a particular request for sharing or access to a work.
- **Technical Debt**: Short-term compromises made during development that create a long-term burden.
- **Automatic Distribution**: Process requiring no human intervention for work distribution.
- **Public Domain**: The domain where no copyright applies, allowing free access to works.

### E
- **Borrowing**: Action of renting a copyrighted digital work for a determined period.
- **Shared Disk Space**: Constraint on storage organization by members enabling automatic distribution.
- **Cross Evaluation**: Process where pairs evaluate each other's work to develop critical thinking.

### F
- **Markdown Format**: Constraint on the format of output files in the library.
- **PDF Format**: Constraint on the format of input files in the library.

### G
- **Copy Management**: Ability to control and administer copies of works according to legislation.
- **Rights Management**: Ability to administer copyrights on each work.
- **Process Management**: The action of knowing how to introduce and maintain an organizational process.

### L
- **Current Legislation**: The law that applies in each country regarding copyright and intellectual property.

### M
- **Member**: User of the digital library with access and contribution rights.
- **Moderation**: Process of verifying compliance of deposited works with regulations (verification, metadata enrichment, validation or rejection).
- **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)**: Minimum viable version of a product containing essential functionalities.

### N
- **Digitize**: The action of putting a physical document into digital form.

### O
- **Work**: A product from an artist that can be a book, music, video or article.

### P
- **Pair Programming**: Collaborative development practice where two developers work together on the same code.
- **Two-week Period**: Temporal constraint on the rental of copyrighted digital works.
- **Enable Download**: The action of authorizing the download of a work to the user's device.
- **Intellectual Property**: The laws and rules governing the right to manage a work, mainly governed by copyright.

### R
- **Text Recognition**: Use of specific software (AI) to recognize and extract text from an image or scanned document.
- **Resolved**: The action of solving a problem or situation.

### S
- **Repository Structure**: Organization in metaphorical directories:
  - `fond_commun`: public domain works
  - `emprunts`: copyrighted works borrowed and encrypted
  - `séquestre`: copyrighted works pending with restricted access
  - `a_moderer`: proposed works awaiting validation

### U
- **Library User**: A person who uses the digital library services.

### V
- **Validation**: Process of approving a work by a librarian after moderation.
